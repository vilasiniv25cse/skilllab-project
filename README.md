DIGITAL MEMBERSHIP MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

1. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Managing memberships manually can be time-consuming and difficult. Member details, membership plans, payments, and membership status may be stored in different places, making it difficult to track and update information. Members may also face problems checking their membership details and expiry dates.

The Digital Membership Management System provides a simple platform to manage member registration, membership plans, payments, and membership status digitally.

2. PROTOTYPE

Page 1 – Login
• Separate login for Member and Admin
• Username/Email
• Password
• Login button

Page 2 – Member Registration
• Member name
• Email
• Phone number
• Membership plan
• Registration button

Page 3 – Membership Plans
• Available membership plans
• Plan duration
• Plan price
• Plan benefits
• Select plan option

Page 4 – Membership Status
• Member details
• Current membership plan
• Start date
• Expiry date
• Active/Expired status

Page 5 – Payment
• Selected membership plan
• Amount
• Payment method
• Payment status
• Payment confirmation

Page 6 – Admin Dashboard
• Total members
• Active memberships
• Expired memberships
• Payment details
• Manage member records

3. PROBLEM SOLUTION

The Digital Membership Management System solves the problems of manual membership management by providing a centralized digital platform. It allows members to register, select membership plans, make payments, and check their membership status easily.

Admins can manage member information, monitor active and expired memberships, and track payments from one dashboard.

KEY BENEFITS

• Reduces manual paperwork
• Saves time
• Easy member registration
• Easy membership tracking
• Secure and organized records
• Simple payment tracking
• Helps admins manage memberships efficientlyDESIGN SS
